Meet Chef
Solène

Food, for me, is the ultimate form of connection—a shared experience that transcends the ordinary. My culinary philosophy honors the integrity of each ingredient, sourcing exclusively from local artisans and organic farms in their peak season. This dedication to quality is married with the refined elegance of French technique, transforming the raw beauty of nature into a sophisticated sensory journey tailored specifically for your table.

From Provence
to Your Plate

My story began in the sun-drenched kitchens of Provence, learning the rhythms of the seasons from my grandmother. This foundation led me to the rigorous halls of the Institut Culinaire de Paris, followed by years honing my craft in Michelin-starred establishments across Europe. Cuisine de Solène is the culmination of this journey—a personal venture to bring the artistry and intimacy of fine dining directly to you.
